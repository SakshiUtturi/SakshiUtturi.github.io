# Sakshi Utturi: Product Portfolio

My product management portfolio, published with GitHub Pages.

**Live site:** https://sakshiutturi.github.io

## About

I'm a full-stack software engineer at Clarivate Analytics in Bangalore and an aspiring product manager. This site brings together my product work, background and resume in one place.

## What's on the site

- **Case studies:** product problems I've worked through, covering research, strategy, growth, metrics and building with AI.
- **Background:** my engineering experience and skills.
- **Resume:** a link to my current resume.
- **Contact:** email, LinkedIn and GitHub.

The case studies are independent projects based on public research, interviews I conducted, and my own point of view. They are not work done for the companies named.

## How the site is built

- A single `index.html` file with its styles and a few lines of script included. There is no build step and no framework.
- Case studies open and close in place, so the page stays short.
- It works on phones and follows the visitor's light or dark setting.

To view it locally, download `index.html` and open it in a browser.
